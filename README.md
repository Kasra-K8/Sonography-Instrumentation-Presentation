# Sonography: Principles, Instrumentation, and Imaging Modalities

This repository contains a graduate-level course presentation on **Sonography (Ultrasound Imaging)** prepared as part of the **Instrumentation (ابزار دقیق)** course during my M.Sc. studies in Biomedical Engineering.

The presentation provides an overview of the physical principles of ultrasound, the major components of ultrasound imaging systems, and several commonly used ultrasound imaging modalities.

## Presentation Overview

The presentation covers the following topics:

- Fundamentals of ultrasound imaging
- Physical principles of ultrasonic waves
- Frequency, wavelength, intensity, and propagation speed
- Interaction of ultrasound waves with biological tissue
- Reflection, refraction, scattering, and absorption
- Acoustic impedance
- Impedance mismatch and wave transmission
- Ultrasound attenuation and penetration depth
- Effect of tissue density and propagation velocity
- Ultrasound transducers and piezoelectric elements
- Signal processing in ultrasound systems
- Internal architecture of ultrasound imaging systems
- Ultrasound system controls and user interface
- Coupling gel and acoustic transmission
- 2D ultrasound imaging
- 3D ultrasound imaging
- 4D ultrasound imaging
- Elastography
- Doppler ultrasound
- Doppler effect and blood-flow assessment
- Color Doppler
- Spectral Doppler

## Topics

### Ultrasound Physics

The first part of the presentation introduces the physical principles governing ultrasound propagation, including:

- Mechanical longitudinal waves
- Frequency and wavelength
- Propagation velocity
- Reflection and refraction
- Scattering
- Absorption
- Attenuation
- Acoustic impedance

The relationship between tissue properties and ultrasound propagation is also discussed, particularly the effects of density, elasticity, and acoustic impedance on image formation.

### Ultrasound Instrumentation

The presentation also reviews the major components of an ultrasound imaging system, including:

- Ultrasound transducer
- Piezoelectric elements
- Signal processing unit
- Analog-to-digital conversion
- Beamforming
- Display system
- Power supply
- User controls and interface
- Coupling gel

### Ultrasound Imaging Modalities

Several ultrasound imaging techniques are introduced and compared:

- **2D Ultrasound**
- **3D Ultrasound**
- **4D Ultrasound**
- **Elastography**
- **Doppler Ultrasound**

### Elastography

Elastography is discussed as an ultrasound-based technique for evaluating tissue stiffness and elasticity. The presentation includes examples of its use in assessing tissue abnormalities and liver fibrosis.

### Doppler Ultrasound

The final section introduces the Doppler effect and its application to blood-flow assessment.

Topics include:

- Doppler frequency shift
- Blood-flow direction
- Blood-flow velocity
- Color Doppler
- Spectral Doppler
- Carotid blood-flow assessment
